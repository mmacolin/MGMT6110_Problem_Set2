2. My findings

Finding 1

Where: https://problemset2.vercel.app/ — comparison form and error card.

What I did, what I saw: I selected 1950 for Cambodia and Singapore and clicked “Compare GDP Per Capita.” After the loading state, the page showed “Provider Unreachable” and “Request to World Bank API timed out after 10000ms,” with a Retry request button. I later selected Korea, Dem. People's Rep. as Country B for 2025 and saw the same timeout. A 2023 Cambodia–Singapore request had succeeded earlier, so I cannot tell whether these failures are intermittent or specific to those inputs.

Which heuristic: 1 — Visibility of system status.

Screen or system: System. The screen reports the error and offers Retry, but it cannot make a timed-out data request succeed with information already on the screen.

Severity, and why: 3 — Major. A failed request blocks the product's main comparison task; it happened in two tested cases, though I have not established how often it happens.

The repair: Supported comparisons return within a reasonable time. If the upstream service fails, the app explains the temporary failure clearly and gives a useful retry path without presenting the failure as a missing data value.

Finding 2

Where: https://problemset2.vercel.app/ — comparison form and result area.

What I did, what I saw: I compared Cambodia and Singapore for 2023 and saw two values and their difference. I changed the year to 1950. The completed result disappeared immediately and the area reverted to “Ready to compare,” before I ran a new request.

Which heuristic: 3 — User control and freedom.

Screen or system: Screen. The page already has the last result and the newly selected input, so it could keep the result visibly labeled as the previous comparison until the next request completes.

Severity, and why: 2 — Minor. I could run the old comparison again, but changing one input removes information I may need while deciding what to compare next.

The repair: Keep the previous result visible with its original countries and year, mark it as the prior result when inputs change, and replace it only after the next comparison succeeds.

Finding 3

Where: https://problemset2.vercel.app/ — result cards and provenance line beneath the comparison.

What I did, what I saw: After the 2023 Cambodia–Singapore comparison, I saw the indicator code and a “Retrieved at” time. The World Bank link went to the general indicator page; the result did not provide a direct link or precise source reference for the two selected countries and year, and the retrieval time showed no date.

Which heuristic: 10 — Help and documentation.

Screen or system: Screen. The selected country codes, year, indicator code, and retrieval timestamp are already available in the result, so the page can display a complete citation or source link.

Severity, and why: 2 — Minor. I can read the comparison, but checking or citing its exact underlying observations takes extra work.

The repair: Each result provides an exact country, indicator, and year source link or citation, plus the full retrieval date and time with a time zone.

3. My predictions

The three findings I expect my groupmates to raise, and the severity I expect for each: Finding 1 (3), because a timeout interrupts the main task if they encounter it; Finding 2 (2), because the result vanishes as soon as they change an input; and Finding 3 (2), because the general source link is easy to notice when they try to verify a value. Finding 1 depends on an intermittent failure, so it is less certain they will reproduce it.

The heuristic I think my product breaks worst: 1 — Visibility of system status. The timeout message says the provider is unreachable but does not tell the user whether to retry later, choose another input, or expect the same failure again.

The finding that would show my own evaluation was wrong: A groupmate might find that the country selector or result is clipped or unusable on a phone and rate it 3 or 4. I tested in desktop Chrome only, so I may have missed a more serious mobile issue.
